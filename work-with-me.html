Game Marketing Built Around What You're Trying to Accomplish

Benne Media Solutions provides PR and marketing strategy and execution for indie game developers, tabletop creators, and other creator-led projects.

You shouldn't need to diagnose your own marketing problem before seeking help.

Start with what you're trying to accomplish. We'll determine which combination of PR, Steam strategy, creator outreach, community building, content, email, research, paid campaigns, or launch support belongs inside the solution.

How We Can Help

Game Launch Strategy

Build a clear marketing roadmap around your development timeline, launch goals, audience, platforms, messaging, and available resources.

Steam Visibility & Wishlist Strategy

Improve how players discover, understand, and respond to your game before launch, with strategy focused on your Steam presence and wishlist opportunities.

Creator & Influencer Outreach

Identify the right creators for your game and build outreach around genuine audience fit instead of chasing the biggest follower counts.

Gaming PR & Press Strategy

Build stronger pitches, press materials, media targeting, and outreach strategies designed around how gaming journalists evaluate stories.

Community & Audience Building

Turn awareness into connection by building systems that help potential players become followers, community members, advocates, and eventually customers.

Marketing Audits & Consulting

Already doing marketing but unsure what's working? We'll identify the gaps, opportunities, and highest-priority changes before you spend more time or money.
