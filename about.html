Professional Bio

I'm a marketing journalist, launch strategist, and founder of Benne Media Solutions. For over a decade, I've worked across gaming, media, events, creator communities, and grassroots culture helping products and people get seen.

Great work gets buried. Not because people don't care. Because nobody helped the audience connect to it. That's the gap I focus on.

I help developers, creators, and brands build marketing systems that feel intentional, sustainable, and human — not loud for the sake of attention.

No fake hype. No trend chasing. No bloated agency pricing. Just strategy designed to help people discover what you've already built.
