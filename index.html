Indie Game Marketing Built Around Discovery

Benne Media Solutions is a video game marketing and PR consultancy helping indie game developers and growing studios build audiences before, during, and after launch.

Led by marketing journalist and launch strategist Junae Benne, we help teams turn great games into discoverable ones through indie game marketing strategy, Steam visibility, creator and influencer outreach, gaming PR, community building, and launch planning.

Whether you're preparing for your first Steam launch, trying to grow wishlists, looking for press and creator coverage, or realizing a little later than you'd like that your game needs an audience, we'll help you figure out what comes next.

Great work gets buried. Not because people don't care. Because nobody helped the audience connect to it. That's the gap I focus on.
